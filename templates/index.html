About Glory

A gathering shaped by worship.

ECWA Glory Concert is a free, all-night Gospel music concert featuring exceptional psalmists from Nigeria and around the world. Convened annually by ECWA Lagos West DCC, it creates a place where people can raise their hearts, hands and voices together in praise.
