About the Concert

‘Ecwa Glory Concert’ is a free, all-night Gospel music concert featuring exceptional psalmists from Nigeria and around the world. Convened annually by Ecwa Lagos West DCC (LWDCC). Birthed by a God-inspired vision, ‘Ecwa Glory Concert’ provides a platform where all can raise their hearts, hands and voices together as one in unfettered praise to the Almighty irrespective of tribe, denomination, creed or language.
