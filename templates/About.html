About the concert

Our story

A song of worship, sustained by grace.

ECWA Glory Concert is a free, all-night gospel music gathering created to bring believers together in unified worship. Since its beginning, it has become a space where people from different backgrounds come as one family to lift their voices, open their hearts, and celebrate the goodness of God.

Every year, the event carries the same central purpose: to make room for prayer, praise, unity, and spiritual refreshment. It is a gathering shaped by worship and anchored in the local church community, but opened to all who desire to experience the presence of God.

History

Founded in faith

Established by ECWA Lagos West DCC, the event has grown into a major gospel gathering that brings people together in worship, fellowship, and thanksgiving.

Mission

Lift Christ higher

To create a generous and welcoming platform for worship that rises above division and joins every voice into one spirit of praise.

Experience

Open to all

Free entry, warm hospitality, and a spirit of love, prayer, and worship make the gathering accessible for everyone.

The gathering
